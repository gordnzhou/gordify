1. Enter your URL or query, then click 'Add to my library'. Note that Playlists URLs must be public. A download job will be started, which you can view the status of.

2. A job typically less than a minute for single song requests, but can take over twenty minutes for playlists with 100+ songs.

3. Wait for your job's status to be 'finished'. You can see your new tracks by logging in on any music client (Symfonium, Arpeggi, etc..) with the same credentials, then ensure your music client is synced (sync should be automatic if on the VPN).

4. If any songs failed to download, run the same query again at a later time to retry. Note that some songs will fail no matter how many times you retry.
